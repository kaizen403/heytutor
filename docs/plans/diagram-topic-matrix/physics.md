## Read and use the matrix

Use [physics.csv](physics.csv) as the complete machine-readable assignment record. Chapter names, topic labels, `unit_id` and `topic_id` are copied exactly from `data/question-bank/syllabus-taxonomy.json`; even questionable and unlisted labels remain visible. Tables follow the root plan's Physics core priority order, leaving the intervening Mathematics positions intact. A `CH` ID is a human ownership slice, never a runtime selection or topic-template key.

The source baseline is the official **2026** syllabus. `J-PDF`, `A-PDF`, and `N-PDF` source keys and URLs are defined by the [source index](index.md#primary-sources). References below use physical PDF pages and the named unit/subsection: Main Paper 1 only, pp.1–11; Advanced Physics pp.13–16, with Chemistry p.1 explicitly named where speed distributions cross the subject boundary; NEET Physics pp.4–8. Taxonomy `framework_ids` were not used as scope proof. An unlisted judgement cites the checked relevant inventory; it is not a claim that an exam can never use the concept.

Scope tags are `listed` for explicitly stated content, `application` for a teaching/exam variant of a stated concept, `supplemental` for an unlisted standalone/outside-baseline topic, and `review` where a mixed label or source interpretation needs an editorial decision. A mixed label receives `review` when counting the whole label as explicitly listed would overstate scope. All three exams have Physics, so no Physics row uses `not_applicable`.

Visual need (`required`, `conditional`, `text_only`) is a planning hypothesis, to be independently confirmed at S0 evaluation freeze. `required` describes the proposed family obligations, not every possible recall question; `conditional` depends on the full source; `text_only` still needs an independent reason. None permits removing an eligible miss. The per-topic equations, enumeration, boundary/topology checks and invalid cases below are acceptance obligations, not test results. Apply the root plan's universal packet contract to any later implementation: faithful source, independent oracle, no partial invalid scene, rendered/live/replay evidence, and unchanged denominators.

## Scope decisions and source additions

- **Electrostatics:** CH-08b owns the charged-ring axial integral separately from point charges and Gaussian shell/line/sheet cases. CH-08c separately owns conductor charge sharing and a floating conducting slab, with battery state, grounding, capacitance assumptions and energy accounting explicit. Their `application` scope does not misquote a new syllabus heading.
- **Optics:** optical fibre is a TIR application in CH-10a. CH-10c separately retains YDSE one-arm slab/path shifts and angular fringe width as applications, while standalone thin-film interference and microscope/telescope resolving power are supplemental in all three baselines. Advanced expressly limits interference to YDSE. Main/NEET explicitly list microscopes/telescopes; Advanced does not enumerate those instrument headings.
- **Advanced extensions:** sound Doppler is listed only in Advanced; 1D forced/damped oscillation and resonance are also explicitly listed there but have no dedicated taxonomy row. CH-27c's later evaluation must add versioned source cases for driven response, decay envelope and resonance without creating invented stable IDs in this matrix. Check the independent driven-oscillator equation, energy loss and phase/resonance limits. Main/NEET's A²-time pendulum dissipation experiment remains its own listed activity, not general damped-theory scope. Sources: A-PDF pp.13–14 Mechanics; J-PDF pp.4/6 U10/20; N-PDF pp.5/7 U10/20.
- **Thermal and fluid limits:** Carnot, Newton cooling and Stefan are explicitly Advanced, not separately enumerated Main/NEET headings; refrigerator/heat-pump is a declared second-law application only for Advanced here. Advanced also explicitly lists blackbody absorptive/emissive powers, Kirchhoff and Wien, which lack dedicated taxonomy IDs: attach versioned cases to CH-19d at evaluation freeze and check radiative balance, emissivity/absorptivity relation and λmaxT independently. Advanced conduction is 1D and first-law applications are ideal-gas-only. Poiseuille is expressly excluded there; any non-Newtonian constitutive law or nonlinear pendulum extension needs separately approved supplemental scope and must remain visible as such. Sources: A-PDF p.14 Mechanics / Thermal Physics; J-PDF pp.3–4 U7/8; N-PDF p.5 U7/8.
- **Modern physics and electronics:** radioactive decay is listed in Advanced, supplemental in Main/NEET. Advanced α/β/γ radiation, mean life, characteristic/continuous X-rays and Moseley lack separate stable taxonomy rows but remain CH-23c source obligations: check decay equations and radiation conservation, bremsstrahlung cutoff λmin=hc/(eV), and the supplied Moseley Z/screening law independently. EM-spectrum X-rays in CH-26b do not satisfy these. Main/NEET semiconductor devices and five logic gates are listed; Advanced has no electronic-device heading. Transistors/amplifiers and their lab activities are retained supplemental rows, not restored current units. Sources: A-PDF p.16 Modern Physics; J-PDF p.6 U18/19; N-PDF p.7 U18/19.
- **Experimental method boundaries:** Main/NEET enumerate 18 activities; their multiple taxonomy grains remain individually assigned. Advanced's General list instead includes g from pendulum, liquid specific heat by calorimeter, concave-mirror/convex-lens **u–v**, and wire specific resistance by **metre bridge and post office box**. The parallax taxonomy rows carry separate Advanced u–v variants, not a false claim that Advanced lists parallax. The resistivity row carries a separate post-office-box arm/plug/null observation obligation. A no-parallax coincidence, u–v equation fit, and post-office-box bridge balance are different evidence contracts. Sources: J-PDF p.6 U20 activities 1–18; N-PDF pp.7–8 U20 activities 1–18; A-PDF p.13 General.
- **Editorial reviews stay in the denominator:** the exact “Simple pendulum dissipation of energy by plotting the L-T^2 graph” label conflates the g/period relation with energy loss. It stays `review` in all exams until the label/expected source interpretation is decided; the taxonomy itself is unchanged. Solenoid/toroid and circular-motion/cyclotron mixed labels, and partly enumerated Advanced modern-physics labels, likewise retain their explicit scope qualifications.

These source additions are not extra taxonomy assignments or present passes. The integration owner must reconcile them into the frozen evaluation ledger with stable source-case IDs before claiming official-syllabus completeness. Supplemental and review rows remain part of the 342-topic assignment denominator even when a future exam-specific required denominator differs.
