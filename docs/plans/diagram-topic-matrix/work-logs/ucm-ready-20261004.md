## Admission and numeric authority (coordinator rules, 4 October 2026)

The family is admitted only when the turn plan or ProblemIR names circular
motion (a law tag, a symbol a_c, ω or F_c, an angular rate unit, or a
ProblemIR fact). The question is then read only for its quantities. Any
plan value whose unit and symbol bind without doubt to one recomputed
quantity is corrected through the shared `applySourceQuantityAuthority` seam
(one call in `useQuestionHandler`, also running the circuit authority), so
narration reads the corrected number; a conflict that binds to nothing
declines the figure (`stale_plan_quantity`). The mock planner and the
fallback plan emit no law tags: the topic is not admitted there, and for
stems that bind a radius and a rate (or a degenerate radius) the legacy
static circle, stock tangent with F_c, and r = 0 circle are declined rather
than drawn. The native Q37 stem binds no numbers, so under a mock plan it
still gets the legacy `circular_motion_level` figure. Numbers use the shared
`STEM_NUMBER` grammar.

## Declared READY scope

One body in uniform circular motion with a positive radius from a stated
radius, diameter, string of a horizontal circle, or a position about the
centre; at least one rate (speed, angular speed in rad/s or rad/ms, rpm,
rev/s, Hz, period, N revolutions in t, centripetal acceleration); optional
mass, elapsed time, sense and position (Cartesian about the centre or a
compass point). Symbolic R, v, θ stems draw a qualitative figure.
