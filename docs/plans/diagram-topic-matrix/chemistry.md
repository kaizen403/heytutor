## Scope boundaries that change acceptance

- Henry's law is explicitly J/N U6 and A Solutions; its taxonomy U5 placement is preserved. Main/NEET explicitly name nonideal solution plots, while A Solutions names ideal solutions only.
- J/N p-block U10 lists configuration/trends/first-element anomalies. The six group-compound rows retain their IDs but their deeper named species are A-explicit and J/N scope-review/supplemental depth, not proof of shared named-compound scope.
- U13 qualitative analysis includes N/S/**P**/halogens and quantitative basic principles include C/H/N/halogens/S/**P**. U20 extra-element tests name only N/S/halogens. A organic formula determination is combustion-method only; A Practical Organic additionally names nitro detection.
- J/N names **ethane Sawhorse and Newman**. A names **ethane and butane Newman only**, limits enantiomer/diastereomer/meso relationships to at most two asymmetric centres and excludes R/S and E/Z configurations. Its stated addition/elimination stereochemistry restriction and Haloarenes benzyne/cine exclusion remain; its Alkyl Halides substitution stereochemical aspects remain included. J/N broad stereoisomerism is not an automatic exhaustive CIP requirement.
- A MO scope is homonuclear diatomics through Ne2; hybridisation uses s/p/d only; wavefunction/density treatment is plots-only; thermodynamic work is PV only. A named CFT fields are octahedral/tetrahedral, even though candidate code also models square-planar splitting.
- J/N soluble-salt analysis excludes insoluble salts. A uses a different ion roster, preserves Hg2+ as printed, adds bicarbonate and excludes fluoride; do not transfer J/N Ni2+/NH4+/nitrite into that explicit A list. C-20 rows retain all six preparations, all three titration categories and all four activities (including both sol types) without extra taxonomy rows.
